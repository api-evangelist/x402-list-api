FilingDelta sells JSON cards that say what changed: material changes in a company's latest SEC filing compared with the last one, and central-bank rate decisions with the vote and dissents where the bank publishes them. Each change carries a materiality rating, a citation and the EDGAR or central-bank source link. It's a change log, not a stock tip.
